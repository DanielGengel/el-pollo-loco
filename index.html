Tipps


Collect bottles and coins first. Bottles can defeat enemies, but do not throw them while running, because you might hit yourself and lose energy or even die. Normal enemies need 1 bottle hit. The endboss needs 5 bottle hits. Your character can also take 5 hits before dying. You can store only 5 bottles, so collect them before fighting the endboss and use them carefully. Coins can heal you: if you lost energy and collected 5 coins, you get 20% energy back. If your energy is still full, the 5 coins do not heal you, so collect and use everything wisely.
